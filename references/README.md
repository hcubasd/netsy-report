# Scientific references

`references.bib` and `metadata.json` are inherited from the SLR as a reference
pool. Their presence does not assert that every source is cited or needed in
the research article. Curate the selection with the actual scientific question.
The SLR's query, access and appraisal ledgers remain on v0.2.0.

Venue policies and structural examples are documented separately in
`docs/venue/`; source PDFs and snapshots remain outside Git. The final journal
bibliography workflow and flattened source export are not implemented yet.
