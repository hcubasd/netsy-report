\begin{abstract}
Freight network-load and emissions synthesis connects economic activity,
logistics decisions, vehicle operations and road-network use. This review maps
those foundations across seven domains, including data availability and
transferability. It synthesizes the currently accessible literature and official
guidance, with MASS-GT as a designated reference configuration. The evidence
supports established consistency principles alongside context-dependent
formulations and uneven validation. Documented data configurations combine
public statistics, restricted records and estimated information; their usable
stratification cannot be inferred from nominal data availability alone. The
search and selection record is reported explicitly, including bounded retrieval
and outstanding full-text access. NetSy's implementation choices are deferred.
\end{abstract}
