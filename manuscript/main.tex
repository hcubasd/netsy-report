\begin{abstract}
Freight network-load and emissions synthesis connects economic activity,
logistics decisions, vehicle operations and road-network use. This review maps
those foundations through eight search strands and synthesizes the findings
around seven functional responsibilities, from study-area representation to
environmental assessment. Six subject strands are complemented by separate
searches on data feasibility and the designated MASS-GT reference. The synthesis
integrates methodological alternatives, their information requirements and the
reference configuration within each responsibility. The evidence supports
established consistency principles alongside context-dependent formulations and
uneven validation. Public statistics, restricted records and estimated inputs
support different levels of representation; nominal data availability does not
establish usable detail. The search and selection record remains explicit,
including bounded retrieval and outstanding full-text access. NetSy's
implementation choices are deferred.
\end{abstract}
