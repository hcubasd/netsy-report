\section{Exploratory results}
\subsection{Fixed-reference correspondence}
The refreshed control assigns the existing MASS-GT reference tours, parcel
schedule and service matrices using NetSy v0.5.0, rather than comparing
independently synthesized trip sets. Across 158,806 directed links, mean
absolute load difference is 0.00107 trips, root mean squared difference is
0.0649 trips and maximum absolute difference is 8.467 trips. These are
conditional correspondence measures, not traffic-count validation.

Inherited-factor CO$_2$ totals are 16,792.874\,t for the source and
16,795.387\,t for NetSy on those same source trips. The 2.513\,t difference
is accounted for by the documented source urban-parcel accumulation correction;
the remaining aggregate residual is 0.248\,g. This accounting isolates the implementation repair from the scheduling and
emissions alternatives considered next.

\subsection{Terrain coverage}
Table~\ref{tab:terrain} reports the retained terrain grades and explicit
fallbacks. Accepted links span 53,866 directed kilometres, compared with
86,402 directed physical-road kilometres in the input network, including
external connections. Among accepted links, the 1st and 99th grade percentiles
are $-1.993$\% and $+2.006$\%; the median is effectively zero. Coverage counts
are not a substitute for vehicle-kilometre-weighted exposure.
