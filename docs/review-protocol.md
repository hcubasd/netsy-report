# NetSy review protocol — available-evidence revision

Version: 0.2, 18 November 2026. Branch: v0. Status: active, bounded review with institutional retrieval pending. This protocol governs reassessment and synthesis from this point; it does not retrospectively govern the exploratory searches of 11–16 November. Amendments must be dated and justified.

## Purpose and questions
Map established formulations, credible alternatives, empirical support and information conditions for synthesizing freight network loads and emissions. NetSy is the intended application; its existing implementation is not an inclusion criterion. MASS-GT is a designated reference configuration, not a standard of truth or an eighth domain.

- RQ1: Which modelling approaches address the seven domains, and what theoretical, comparative and validation evidence supports them?
- RQ2: Which data configurations, classifications and resolutions support these approaches across study areas, and what adaptations and limitations arise when information is missing?
- RQ3: Which formulations, data and assessment evidence define the MASS-GT reference within each domain?

Concept: freight demand, logistics decisions, vehicle activity, road-network use and environmental effects, including their enabling data. Context: urban, metropolitan and regional applications; broader economic/network foundations when they support these outcomes. Study areas are application contexts, not a population assumed to have homogeneous data.

## Evidence identification
Existing OpenAlex/Crossref retrieval and explicit supplements are the available search frame. Preserve dates, engine-specific syntax, limits, refinements and raw responses. Domain queries remain independent of the model name. Separate MASS-GT discovery uses model-name searches, official bibliography and targeted citation tracing. Official data documentation and the advisor paper have explicit supplementary/designated routes. No exhaustive citation-search or database coverage claim.

The initial collection contained 50 reading-collection PDFs and two designated local documents. Eleven relevant complete PDFs obtained through institutional access on 18 November replace two one-page placeholders and extend the current cohort to 61 complete documents (59 domain sources, two background sources). One unrelated syllabus was excluded. Methodological guidance is separate. Full-text eligibility is assessed within this available cohort; it is not a substitute for screening all database records. Unexamined database records remain deferred. Outstanding documents remain awaiting retrieval rather than being excluded for lack of access. No new topical search is introduced in this revision.

## Eligibility and selection
Include identifiable journal papers, conference papers, author versions/preprints, theses, foundational books and official technical documents that provide an inspectable formulation, empirical assessment or data specification relevant to at least one domain. Record evidence type and publication/implementation version.

Use generic algorithm exposition and discovery compendia as contextual sources rather than substantive domain evidence. Exclude subject mismatches from the synthesis. Do not infer empirical support from a conceptual proposal. Partial PDFs/abstracts identify sources but cannot support method-performance findings. Literature in English is the principal search frame; Swedish official documentation is eligible and inspected in its source language. No assertion of complete multilingual coverage.

Apply eligibility to each held document. Record inclusion/context-only/awaiting-full-text, reason, source identity and actual reading extent. Availability is a reporting attribute, not a claim that unavailable studies lack merit. Final selection remains open to university-supplied documents.

## Extraction and appraisal
For each full-text source extract: object/outcome; formulation; actors and boundaries; data inputs and roles; classification/resolution; public/restricted/borrowed/synthetic information; estimation/calibration/validation design; results; limits of inference and transfer. Use targeted complete relevant sections rather than assume cover-to-cover reading.

Assess conceptual definition, empirical basis, comparison fairness, independent/out-of-sample/cross-area validation, identification, uncertainty and data access. A descriptive review, normative solver, calibrated model, observed-data validation and official data manual answer different questions. No arbitrary pooled quality score or cross-model accuracy ranking. Shared source data and research programmes are not independent votes.

AI-assisted extraction and synthesis are performed by one assistant; no independent second screening/extraction is claimed. Author/advisor verification remains necessary before submission. Preserve page-level evidence notes and flags for incomplete reading.

## Synthesis and MASS-GT anchor
Narrative synthesis by seven domains; distinguish established principles, established alternatives and unresolved questions. Provide a source-linked MASS-GT reference paragraph in each. The 2025 full-model paper, earlier applications and manual at commit 4f727c3633bf5edabc1b0d0fa081a9e232bf169a are separately versioned: neither publication date nor model name proves correspondence to that checkout.

Data availability is both a domain and a cross-domain extraction field. Separate collected information, published aggregates, restricted microdata, operational inputs, estimated parameters, and calibration/validation observations. Do not claim availability in most regions from a convenience sample of documented applications.

Do not position or redesign NetSy's implementation, claim causal policy validity from fit, or call MASS-GT agreement independent validation. No meta-analysis is planned because objectives, evidence designs and outcomes differ.

## Reporting and amendments
Kitchenham–Charters informs protocol/conduct; PRISMA-ScR informs reporting; PRISMA-S informs search documentation. These are guidance sources, not a compliance certification. One appendix records strategy, full strings, selection accounting, supplementary routes, outstanding access and limitations. Main methods summarizes the process. Report the bounded scope and incomplete record screening plainly.
